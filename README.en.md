# TameWheel

[简体中文](README.md) | **English**

[Download for macOS (public preview)](https://github.com/gitman-g1/TameWheel/releases/tag/v0.3.0) · [Installation guide](docs/install.en.md)

**A more comfortable mouse wheel. The same natural trackpad feel.**

TameWheel is a lightweight macOS menu bar app that gives your mouse a familiar scrolling direction and consistent, fixed-step scrolling while preserving your trackpad's natural feel.

- **Familiar direction**: Reverses mouse-wheel scrolling independently. Keep Natural Scrolling enabled in macOS while your mouse follows Windows conventions.
- **Consistent scrolling**: A fixed step size without scroll acceleration, so there's no speed setting to fine-tune.
- **Trackpad unchanged**: Preserves its direction, speed, and momentum. Your mouse and trackpad each feel right.
- **Just one switch**: Turn it on from the menu bar and you're ready to go. Turn it off to restore system scrolling. Your preference is remembered automatically.

Requires macOS 13 or later. A universal package is available for Apple Silicon and Intel. Supports vertical scrolling with standard mouse wheels. Accessibility permission is required on first use.

This preview has not been notarized by Apple. Follow the installation guide to allow the app in System Settings on first launch.
